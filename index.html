About the Brand

MA Casual Refashioning is a CAC-registered Nigerian unisex fashion brand based in Benin City, Edo State.

We make a wide range of male and female outfits, combining personal style, creative design and careful tailoring. Whether a customer wants a custom-made look, a ready-to-wear outfit or help refreshing an existing garment, our aim is to make fashion feel personal and well-finished.

Our promise: thoughtful designs, attention to fit and service for different styles and occasions.
